# BRIEF · Taqdeer Manpower V8

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner asked for the V7 site
to be redone with the scroll-craft skill to look "more premium and modern". No
interview took place. Every decision below was authored. All copy and figures
come from the V7 site, which follows the company profile. No new figures were invented.

1. **Vibe:** precise, two-sided, earned, industrial-calm. References: a shipping
   manifest, a passport stamp, the two-tone TAQDEER | MANPOWER wordmark itself.
2. **Journey:** two sides of the world meet → where it began → scale → how people
   are prepared → trades cross onto landmark sites → reach → method → your project.
3. **Energy curve:** confident open, calm middle, quiet before the peak, a long
   held peak, then a decisive resolve.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment:** see the curve below. The moment: *the trades
   walk across the divider and land on site.*
5. **What no other site does:** the words physically cross the page. Each word
   changes colour exactly as it passes through the seam.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, with an editorial weight to the type.
7. **World:** distinct scenes on one held split. Not one continuous world.
8. **Assets:** no photography or footage. A brand palette (royal blue #274798, gold
   #EEBE0D), the wordmark, and V7's Three.js globe, re-rendered in two tones.

## Grammar
**Split stage** (uniqueness §2.7). The business is literally two-sided: workers are
sourced in one part of the world and deployed in another. SOURCE (limestone,
blue, TAQDEER) and SITE (navy, gold, MANPOWER) hold for the whole page. The
divider is the chrome. The close is the collapse.
