01 — Who we are

Five decades of trust. Built by hand.

Established with humble beginnings in the late 1960s, our Group traces its roots to early manpower and project-support operations in Libya and Iraq, in association with reputed international contractors such as J&P.

By the late 1980s we expanded into Saudi Arabia and the UAE, laying the foundation for long-term partnerships across the Middle East, Europe and beyond — today known for scale, compliance, ethical sourcing and worker welfare.
