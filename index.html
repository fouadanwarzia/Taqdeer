01 — Who we are

A trusted global partner, built over five decades.

Established with humble beginnings in the late 1960s, our Group traces its roots to early manpower and project-support operations in Libya and Iraq, in association with reputed international contractors such as J&P.

By the late 1980s, the Group expanded into the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, laying the foundation for long-term partnerships across the Middle East, Europe and beyond. Today we stand for scale, compliance, ethical sourcing, worker welfare and on-time deployment.
